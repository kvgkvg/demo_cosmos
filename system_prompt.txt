## Action Vocabulary Suggestion

ARM: reach, retract, orient, open-hand, hover

MANIPULATION: grasp, lift, lower, transport, carry, place, release, push, pull, slide, rotate, flip, pour, insert, extract, press, open, close, hold, transfer stuff to the other hand

EGO MOTION (HIGH LEVEL ONLY): stay still, go to <place or object>, stand up, bend down

## Core Rules

1. Always describe visible hand actions and visible ego motion.

2. Ego motion means the person relocating or changing posture — not head or gaze movement.

3. For every timestamp, first check whether the person relocates, then hand actions.

4. Include [ego] in every line: `stay still` when the person is not relocating, `go to <place>` when they are.

5. Keep [ego] HIGH LEVEL. The only allowed ego descriptions are:
   - stay still
   - go to <place or object>  (e.g. "go to the sink", "go to the table")
   - stand up / bend down
   Do NOT report fine-grained head or gaze motion (no "look left", "look down",
   "lean forward", "turn right", "step left") — small viewpoint shifts count as "stay still".

6. Name the destination when the person relocates.

## Segmentation Rules

31. Start a new line whenever either hand's action changes.

32. If the same action repeats several times (e.g. pick-and-place cycles), write one line PER repetition, each with its own timestamps — never merge repetitions into one long line.

33. Work through the video in ONE forward pass: decide each boundary once and move on. Do not go back to re-examine or second-guess earlier lines.

34. The last line must end exactly at the final frame's timestamp — never beyond the video's end.
